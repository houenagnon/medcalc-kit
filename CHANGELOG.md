# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — unreleased
An evaluator of MedCalc-Bench, overall and step by step.
- `mk.evaluate_model(model)`: builds every item, generates (resumable), scores, and returns an `EvaluationReport`:
  `general` (end-to-end accuracy per category), `steps` (E, K, S, A and E2E on the same examples), `by_calculator`,
  `examples` (first failing step of each example), `first_failures`; views saved as CSV.
- The whole benchmark is supported end to end: 55 calculators, 1,100 test examples. `Example` keeps non-numeric
  answers; E2E reads and scores dates (`MM/DD/YYYY` and other formats) and gestational ages (weeks and days), with an
  answer format adapted to each question (`{answer_format}` placeholder).
- `ProbeSuite` builds the step probes (E, K, S, A) only for the 33 equation-based calculators and E2E for every
  example. New `probes.STEP_PROBES`, `generation.model_name`.

Dynamic splits:
- `MedCalcDataset.split_off(name, fraction=/n=, from_split=, exclude_leaks=)` (stratified by calculator,
  reproducible), `folds(k)`, `assign_split(ids, name)`, `with_splits(mapping or CSV)`, `save_splits(path)`,
  `splits()`; `filter` takes lists, `note_type` and `where=` (any condition on the DataFrame).
- `data.concat` / `ds1 + ds2` and `data.from_records` (your own examples); `data.demo(n_per_calculator)`.
- Leakage checks work for any pair of splits: `data.leak_flags`, `data.leaking_rows`; `flag_train_rows` and
  `usable_train_pool` take `train=` and `test=`. `evaluate_model(split=...)` takes custom or several splits, with
  K test points from train examples that do not leak into them.
- The dataset's repr shows its splits: `MedCalcDataset(11638 examples, 55 calculators; train: 10538, test: 1100)`.

Prompts:
- Three wordings of every prompt (`TEMPLATES` in each probe module, `knowledge.PATIENT_TEMPLATES`): "v1" (the
  default, unchanged) and two paraphrases with the same information and answer format; `template="v2"` selects one.
- Items record `template` (name) and `template_hash`; scores have a `template` column.
- `evaluate_model(prompt_variants=3)` and `EvaluationReport.prompt_sensitivity` (accuracy per step and wording, mean,
  sd, range); `EvaluationReport.consistency` (E2E accuracy when every step succeeds / when a step fails).
- Unusable answers (empty, truncated, looping) have their category as `error_type`.
- User guide: section "Prompt design and robustness".

## 0.3.0 — unreleased
Easier to use:
- Top-level shortcuts: `mk.load`, `mk.derive`, `mk.ProbeSuite`, `mk.score`, `mk.reference_answer`, `mk.run_items`,
  `mk.read_records`, `mk.score_records`, `mk.summary`.
- `data.load()` defaults to `data.DEFAULT_REVISION`; `data.demo()` gives four hand-written examples to try everything
  offline; `MedCalcDataset.sample(n, per_calculator=...)`; `Example` prints a one-line summary.
- `run_items(items, path, model)` takes the model directly: an object with `generate_batch` or `generate`, or a
  function; `model_id` is inferred; `generate` may return the text alone.
- `score_records` only needs `item_id` and `text` per answer; `details=True` adds the text and the full verdict;
  K items are collapsed by prompt even without a `prompt_key`. `summary(by=...)` keeps any grouping column.
- `probes.reference_answer(item)` (an answer the scorer accepts, i.e. the expected format) and `probes.prompt(item)`
  (the chat as text); `probes.score` accepts a DataFrame row.
- `calculators.verify_registry(ds)`; clearer errors for an unknown calculator, a missing entity or an unknown probe;
  `ProbeSuite("E")` accepts a single probe name.
- Fix: the E option `fields` given to `ProbeSuite` applied to the first example only.

Documentation:
- Every public function has a docstring with a runnable example, checked by the test suite.
- `docs/guide.md` (user guide), `docs/api.md` (API reference) and `docs/calculators.md` (inputs and a worked example
  per calculator), generated by `docs/build_docs.py`.
- `examples/tutorial.ipynb`: the whole library on the benchmark with Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, executed.

## 0.2.0
Collections are returned as pandas DataFrames, easier to inspect than lists of JSON-like dicts.
- `ProbeSuite.build` returns a DataFrame, one row per item (`as_frame=False`: the former `{label: [item, ...]}`).
- `probes.read_items` returns a DataFrame (`as_frame=False`: a list); `write_items` accepts a DataFrame, a list or a dict.
- New `probes.items_frame` / `probes.as_items` to convert between DataFrames and lists of items.
- New `generation.read_records`: an output file as a DataFrame (`meta` flattened, truncated lines skipped).
- `run_items` and `evaluate.score_records` accept DataFrames of items and records (lists of dicts still work).
- New `Derivation.to_frame()` (inputs, coefficients, steps) and `calculators.table()`.
- `calculators.verify` writes `mismatches.csv` instead of `mismatches.jsonl`.

## 0.1.0 — unreleased
First version, extracted from the research code of a study on medical-calculation competencies of LLMs.
- `data`: MedCalc-Bench-Verified loading at a pinned revision, `Example` / `MedCalcDataset`, leakage and quality control.
- `calculators`: executable registry of the 33 equation-based calculators (reproduces the 660 test answers).
- `probes`: E (extraction), K (formula knowledge), S (substitution), A (arithmetic) and end-to-end, each with
  prompt builder and deterministic scorer.
- `generation`: backend-agnostic resumable runner, generation-artifact classification, optional HF backend.
- `evaluate`: scoring of generation records and per-probe summaries.
- Every setting is a function argument (no global configuration): prompt templates, token budgets, system prompt,
  number formatting, tolerances, K mode (`general` / `patient`), case semantics, test points, artifact thresholds;
  `ProbeSuite` builds named variants of a probe.
